Hi, I’m Quinn. I’ve been a competitive swimmer for 10 years, and I am currently swimming for and studying Kinesiology with a Pre-PT emphasis at Whittier College. I am currently learning and honing my skills as both a swim and strength coach, and I aspire to work as a coach for senior/collegiate athletes, specifically swimmers. I also do photography on the side. I mostly photograph sports, but I have worked in some other areas as well. You can check out my portfolio down below.

In high school I worked on the yearbook team for 4 years, and I served as the Editor in Chief in my senior year. I also participated in VEX robotics on the award winning team 21S where I was a programmer, builder, and designer. Over my 3 years with the team we won 2 Tournaments, and won 5 other awards.
